# Analytics methodology

All calculations are deterministic and scoped to the current selected period.

## Time and activity

- **Total tracked time:** sum of normalized duration minutes.
- **Category/subcategory time:** sum by the exact user-defined label.
- **Share:** category minutes / all tracked minutes.
- **Frequency:** number of normalized records; active days use distinct ISO dates.
- **Average duration:** minutes / record count.
- **Daily, weekly, monthly time:** grouped sums. Weeks begin Monday.
- **Time of day:** start hour grouped into Morning 05:00–11:59, Afternoon 12:00–16:59, Evening 17:00–21:59, and Night otherwise. Missing times default to noon for distribution only.
- **Day of week:** duration grouped by UTC-safe calendar weekday.

## Streaks and gaps

An active day is any date containing a normalized record. Consecutive active dates form a streak. A gap is the number of dates between active dates with no record.

## Consistency

Overall consistency:

```text
55% × active-day frequency
+ 30% × gap regularity
+ 15% × longest-streak strength
```

Category consistency:

```text
50% × category active-day frequency
+ 30% × gap regularity
+ 20% × longest-streak strength
```

Gap regularity is `1 / (1 + standard deviation of active-day gaps)`. Frequency and streak terms are capped at 1. Scores are rounded to 0–100.

## Fragmentation

- A session is one normalized record.
- Short sessions are positive-duration records below 30 minutes.
- Context switching increments when adjacent time-sorted records on the same date have different categories.

These are neutral structural measures and are never labelled productivity.

## Planned completion

Completion rate is completed planned records / all planned records. When there are no planned records, the value is unavailable.

## XP and levels

Category XP visualizes recorded engagement:

```text
duration minutes / 3
+ record count × 8
+ consistency × 2
+ completed planned records × 18
```

Total XP is the sum of category XP. The next level threshold is `round(120 × level^1.55)`. XP does not rank categories morally.

## Associations

For optional energy or mood, LifeMap compares the mean inside a category with the mean across all other category records. Both groups require at least five values. Differences below 0.15 are not presented. Displayed text includes sample size and explicitly states that the association is not evidence of causation.

## Insights

The insight engine has a finite set of calculation-backed statements: top time allocation, dominant start-time bucket, longest recorded session, planned completion, and the strongest qualifying association. Empty space is never filled with motivational copy.

## World, character, and achievements

Analytics feed a separate pure world-generation layer. It uses exact time share, XP, record counts, active days, consistency, streaks, and same-day category transitions to construct districts and paths. Character equipment uses category identity and selected-period share; titles and achievements use finite threshold rules. No world or character feature is generated by an AI model.

Every landmark stores the normalized record IDs supporting it. The application can therefore trace:

```text
world → district → landmark → represented metric → exact normalized records
```

All formulas and thresholds are documented in [WORLD_GENERATION.md](WORLD_GENERATION.md).
